Tenant Flow 

Tenant Flow (Guest)

Step 1: Open the ToLet.city landing page.
Step 2: Click Search Property.
Step 3: Enter the property type, budget, and preferred location.
Step 4: View the property recommendations in the ToLet AI chat.
Step 5: Click View Details to open the selected property.
Step 6: Scroll to view the complete property details.
Step 7: To access the property owner's contact details, 
Step 8: Click Unlock Contact. Register or log in to ToLet, complete the ₹99 payment, and the owner's Name, Mobile Number, and Email ID will be displayed.

Tenant Flow (Login)
Step 1: Open the ToLet.city landing page.
Step 2: Register as a new user by creating a ToLet account, then log in.
Step 3: Click Search Property.
Step 4: Enter the property type, budget, and preferred location.
Step 5: View the property recommendations in the ToLet AI chat.
Step 6: Click View Details to open the selected property.
Step 7: Scroll through the property details.
Step 8: Click Unlock Contact.
Step 9: Complete the ₹99 payment.
Step 10: View the property owner's Name, Mobile Number, and Email ID.

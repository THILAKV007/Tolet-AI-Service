Landlord Flow 

 Account Creation

Step 1: Open the ToLet.city landing page.
Step 2: Scroll down to explore landlord features.
Step 3: Click list your property or Register as landlord. 
Step 4: Register yourself by completing email OTP authentication & verify.
Step 5: Choose your category by landlord.
Step 6: Enter basic details (Name & Mobile number)
Step 7:  Detailed information click No and click proceed as landlord.
Step 8:  Enter your postal code & address 
Step 9:  Upload KYC document for verification 
Step 10 :  Landlord portfolio will open ,You can add property  

Listing Property 
Step 1: Click Add Property.
Step 2: Click Continue to confirm that you are the landlord.
Step 3: Under Basic Information, select the property type: Residential, Commercial, or PG.
Step 4: Select the Property Category/Type.
Step 5: Select the Rent Type (Rent or Lease).
Step 6: Complete all 5 steps of the property listing form.
Step 7: Upload the property images.

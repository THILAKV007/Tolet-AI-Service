Agent Flow

Agent Onboarding Flow

Step 1: Open the ToLet.city landing page.
Step 2: Scroll down to explore the Landlord Features.
Step 3: Click Join as an Agent Partner and select Register.
Step 4: Complete the registration using Email OTP authentication and verify your email.
Step 5: Select Channel Partner as your category.
Step 6: Enter your basic details, including Name and Mobile Number.
Step 7: Under Detailed Information, select Yes and click Proceed as Channel Partner by approving & accepting 50 % brokerage fees.
Step 8: Enter your RERA ID. If you don't have a RERA ID, click Continue Without RERA ID.
Step 9: Enter your Postal Code and Address.
Step 10: Upload the required KYC documents for verification.
Step 11: Once registration is completed, the Broker Portfolio will open, where you can add and manage properties. 

Listing Property 

Step 1: Click Add Property.
Step 2: Click Continue to confirm that you are the landlord.
Step 3: Under Basic Information, select the property type: Residential, Commercial, or PG.
Step 4: Select the Property Category/Type.
Step 5: Select the Rent Type (Rent or Lease).
Step 6: Complete all 5 steps of the property listing form.
Step 7: Upload the property images.
Step 8: Click Submit Listing. After submission, the property will undergo verification and may take up to 24 hours for approval.
